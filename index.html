topic-engine

topic-engine is a private automation tool. Its owner uses it to prepare videos for their own YouTube channels and to upload those videos to those channels.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up for it or use it.

When the owner turns on automatic upload, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload each finished video as private. The owner then reviews it and makes it public by hand.
