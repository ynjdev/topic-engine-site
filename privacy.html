Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who uses this app

topic-engine is a private tool. Its owner (github.com/ynjdev) uses it only with their own Google accounts and their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

When the owner signs in, the app asks Google for permission to manage the signed-in account's YouTube channel. It uses that permission only to:

  • upload videos the owner made, as private
  • read the upload status of those videos
  • make a video public, or delete it, when the owner taps a button to do so
  • read the channel's name, to confirm which channel it is uploading to

It does not read or change anything else on the account.

How the data is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is kept on the owner's own private server and laptop. It is never put in public code. No other personal data from Google is stored.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone. It does not use that data for advertising, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

topic-engine's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the stored token stops working.
